Synopsis

With the wedding of Khemjira and Master Pharan drawing near, their plans are suddenly disrupted when Pharan is called away to investigate the disappearance of a group of people.

At the same time, Khem is asked to look into a string of supernatural disturbances occurring on the set of a series inspired by his own story. Unable to rely on Pharan, he turns to Jettana, Pharan’s most trusted disciple, who volunteers to help deal with the spirits. Chanwit joins them as well, bringing the three longtime friends back together for another dangerous investigation.

As the supernatural activity intensifies, the group must uncover what is haunting the production and find a way to resolve the mystery before it puts Khem in greater danger.

For Jettana and Chanwit, the mission carries an especially urgent goal: protect their friend, survive one final encounter with the supernatural, and make sure Khem returns safely before his long-awaited wedding day.
